Methodological notes

Claim ≠ verified fact

Media reports, hearing notes and public sources are treated as source-attributed claims unless a stronger verification layer is available.

Raw notes stay internal

The public layer is generated from curated exports. Internal raw notes and candidate reconstructions are not exposed by default.

Caveats are part of the interface

Verification status and public caveats should remain visible next to every public reconstruction.
