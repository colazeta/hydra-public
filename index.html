Methodology

Uncertainty is not hidden. It is modelled.

01

Claim ≠ verified fact

Media reports and public sources are treated as attributed claims unless a stronger verification layer is available.

02

Raw notes stay internal

The public layer is generated from curated exports. Internal candidate reconstructions are not exposed by default.

03

Caveats are visible

Verification status, source basis and public caveats are displayed next to public reconstructions.
